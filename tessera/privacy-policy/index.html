Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Tessera (“the app”) is a set of home-screen widgets and wallpapers made by Saral Labs, published as com.dip.tessera. This page explains what the app does with information on your phone.

The short version

  • Tessera has no account, no ads and no analytics.
  • Your moods, focus sessions, chosen apps and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • The only thing Tessera itself sends is a weather request, and only once you add the weather widget or choose a weather place.

Weather

To show the weather, Tessera asks Open-Meteo for the forecast at a place. The place is either a city you search for, or your approximate location if you allow it. When you search, the name you type is sent to Open-Meteo to find matching places. Tessera rounds the location to about one kilometre before sending it and never sends your name, an account or any identifier. Open-Meteo receives the request together with your internet address, as any website does. Tessera stores the last forecast on your phone so the widget can show it without asking again. While a weather widget is on your home screen, the forecast is refreshed about once an hour; removing the last one stops that. If you never add the weather widget or choose a place, no request is made.

Music controls

If you allow notification access, Tessera reads which song is playing so the music widget can show it and offer play, pause and skip. Tessera reads only the media controls of music apps, ignores all other notifications, and stores nothing about what you listen to.

Do not disturb, flashlight and Wi-Fi

The quick toggle widget can switch the flashlight, switch Do not disturb if you allow it, and open the system Wi-Fi panel. It does not read or change anything else.

Wallpapers and export cards

Wallpapers are generated on your phone. When you share an export card, the image goes only to the app you choose to share it with.

Google Play

Google Play checks whether a newer version of Tessera is available, and may show its rating sheet. Google handles this under Google’s privacy policy.

Backups

Your settings, moods and focus streak can be included in your phone’s own backup, which Google encrypts. Tessera has no server and keeps no copy.

Permissions

  • Approximate location (optional) finds the forecast for where you are. Tessera asks only when you choose “Use my approximate location”; you can type a city instead.
  • Notification access (optional) lets the music widget show and control what is playing, as described above.
  • Do not disturb access (optional) lets the quick toggle switch Do not disturb.
  • Set wallpaper applies a Tessera wallpaper when you tap Set.
  • Internet fetches the weather.
  • Run at startup and keep awake briefly let the widgets refresh after a restart and finish redrawing.

Children

Tessera is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
