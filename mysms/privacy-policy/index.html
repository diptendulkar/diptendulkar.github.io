Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

My SMS is a messaging app by Saral Labs, published on Google Play as com.dip.mysms. It replaces your phone’s SMS app and sorts your messages into OTPs, transactions, bills, offers and personal chats. The short version: your messages, and everything My SMS works out from them, stay on your phone.

The short version

  • Your messages, and everything My SMS works out from them, stay on your phone.
  • My SMS has no account, no sign-in and no server. Nothing about your messages or money is uploaded.
  • My SMS has no ads and does not hold Android’s internet permission. Messages you send go through your carrier, and app updates and review prompts come through the Google Play Store app.

What My SMS reads, and why

SMS and MMS. As your default SMS app, My SMS must read, store, send and receive your messages. They stay in Android’s own message store on your phone, where they were before. My SMS reads them to show your conversations, find one-time passwords, and pick out bank transactions, bills and coupon codes.

Spending, bills, subscriptions and coupons. My SMS reads bank and card alerts on your phone to work out your monthly spending by category and by card, card limits, and subscriptions you pay every month. It also finds bill due dates for reminders and keeps coupon codes in a wallet. The results are saved in a private database inside the app on your phone. They are not sent anywhere.

Links. To show where a link in a message really goes, My SMS compares it with a short list of known sites built into the app. Nothing is looked up online.

Contacts. Used only to show names instead of numbers and to suggest recipients. Contacts are read on the phone and never copied out of it.

Phone state. Used only to list your SIM cards, so that on a dual-SIM phone you can choose which one sends.

Notifications. Used to tell you about new messages, upcoming bills, expiring coupons, budgets and, if you turn it on, a weekly spending summary. All of these are worked out on your phone.

Fingerprint or face unlock. Used only if you lock chats in the private vault. Android checks your fingerprint or face; My SMS never sees it. The vault hides chats inside My SMS; it does not encrypt them, and other apps with SMS access can still read them.

Home-screen widgets. If you add the Today or Needs attention widget, it shows your spending, next bill and reminders on your home screen, where anyone holding the unlocked phone can see them.

What leaves your phone

  • Messages you send go through your mobile carrier, as with any SMS app.
  • Picture messages are downloaded and sent by Android’s own MMS service over your carrier’s network.
  • Play services. The Google Play Store app checks for and installs updates to My SMS, and may show a review prompt. Google handles that under its own privacy policy.
  • Things you share. When you send a payment reminder, export a month’s spending as PDF or CSV, or share a message, it goes to the app or place you pick.
  • Backup files. “Back up to a file” and “Export my data” write your money records, bills, coupons, loans and settings to a file in the place you choose, such as your phone’s storage or Google Drive. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private. Messages are not included; they stay in Android’s message store.

What My SMS does not do

  • It does not upload, back up or sync your messages or financial data on its own. A backup file exists only when you make one.
  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • It does not show ads or contain an advertising SDK.
  • It does not include analytics or crash reporting SDKs.
  • It excludes its database from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS, SEND_SMSTo show, receive and send your text messages as the default SMS app.
RECEIVE_MMS, RECEIVE_WAP_PUSHTo receive picture and group messages. Android requires these of every default SMS app.
READ_CONTACTSTo show names instead of numbers and to suggest recipients. Optional.
READ_PHONE_STATETo list your SIM cards so you can choose which one sends.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATEFor new-message alerts and reminders.
USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINTTo unlock the private vault with your fingerprint or face, if you use it.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDSo reminders and scheduled messages survive a restart of the phone.
WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATENot declared by us. Added by Android’s background-work library, which runs the bill reminders and the first scan of your bank messages. It only reads whether a network is present; My SMS has no internet permission.

My SMS does not support RCS chat. Only Google Messages can access RCS on Android.

Money lent and borrowed

Names, amounts and due dates you record under Lent stay on your phone. When you send a reminder, you choose the app it goes to. My SMS doesn’t send it anywhere itself.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling My SMS deletes its database, but not any backup file you saved. Your messages stay in Android’s message store, where your next SMS app will find them. You can delete messages, transactions, bills and coupons in the app at any time. You can also turn on automatic deletion of OTP messages after a day.

Children

My SMS is not directed at children under 13.
