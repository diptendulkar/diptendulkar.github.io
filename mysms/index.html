OTP · Spends · Bills · Split

Your texts, sorted.

My SMS is a messaging app for Android, made for the way texts arrive in India: OTPs, bank alerts, bills, offers and, somewhere in between, messages from people. It becomes your SMS app, copies OTPs in one tap and works out where your money went from the alerts your bank already sends. All of it happens on your phone. There is no account and no server.

What it does

  • OTP in one tap. Copy a one-time password straight from the notification or from the inbox, and optionally delete OTP messages after a day.
  • Personal, transactions, OTPs and offers apart. Messages are sorted using the sender codes Indian businesses must register, plus rules for the wording. Correct a sender once and it stays corrected. Block numbers you never want to hear from.
  • Monthly spending by category. Grocery, food, travel, petrol, shopping, bills and more, worked out from bank and card alerts. Fix a merchant’s category once and it sticks. Add cash spends by hand.
  • Credit cards. Spend per card for the month and the available limit, when your bank reports it.
  • Bill reminders. Card statements, electricity, mobile, broadband and other bills are picked up from the SMS your biller sends, with a reminder before the due date.
  • Coupon wallet. Codes from offer messages, by brand, with expiry dates and a copy button.
  • Split with friends. Groups, equal or exact or percentage or share splits, balances, who pays whom to settle up, and a summary to share. Any card spend can be split in one tap.
  • Everything an SMS app should do. Conversations, MMS, dual SIM, quick reply from the notification, search and contacts.

My SMS does not support RCS chat. Only Google Messages can access RCS on Android.

Where ads appear

One banner on the Money, Coupons and Split screens. There are no ads in conversations, on OTPs, in notifications or during setup, and no full-screen ads. Every ad request asks for non-personalised ads, and nothing from your messages is ever passed to the ad network.

Privacy

Your messages stay in Android’s own message store, and everything My SMS works out from them — spends, bills, coupons, splits — stays in a private database on your phone, excluded from cloud backup. My SMS has no account, no analytics and no crash reporting. It needs the internet for ads, the consent form and Google Play updates. Read the full privacy policy.
