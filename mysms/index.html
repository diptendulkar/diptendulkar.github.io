OTP · Spends · Bills · Lent

Your texts, sorted.

My SMS is a messaging app for Android, made for the way texts arrive in India: OTPs, bank alerts, bills, offers and, somewhere in between, messages from people. It becomes your SMS app, copies OTPs in one tap and works out where your money went from the alerts your bank already sends. All of it happens on your phone. There is no account and no server.

What it does

  • OTP in one tap. Copy a one-time password straight from the notification or from the inbox, hide codes on the lock screen, and optionally delete OTP messages after a day.
  • Personal, transactions, OTPs and offers apart. Messages are sorted using the sender codes Indian businesses must register, plus rules for the wording. Correct a sender once and it stays corrected. Pin, archive, mute or snooze chats, schedule a message, block numbers you never want to hear from, and lock chosen chats in a private vault.
  • Monthly spending by category. Grocery, food, travel, petrol, shopping, bills and more, worked out from bank and card alerts, compared with last month and a budget you set. Name a payee or fix a category once and it sticks. Add cash spends by hand, and export a month as PDF or CSV.
  • Credit cards. Spend per card for the month, the statement and due date, and how much of the limit you have used.
  • Bills and subscriptions. Card statements, electricity, mobile, broadband and other bills are picked up from the SMS your biller sends, with a reminder before the due date, and marked paid when the payment arrives. Subscriptions and AutoPay mandates get a list of their own.
  • Coupon wallet. Codes from offer messages, by brand, with expiry dates, the minimum order, a copy button and a reminder before one lapses.
  • Money lent and borrowed. What friends owe you and what you owe them, with due dates. When someone pays you back by UPI, My SMS offers to mark it against what they owe.
  • Home-screen widgets. Today’s spending and your next bill, and the things that need you soon.
  • Everything an SMS app should do. Conversations, MMS, dual SIM, quick reply from the notification, search and contacts, and a backup file you can restore on a new phone.

My SMS does not support RCS chat. Only Google Messages can access RCS on Android.

No ads

My SMS shows no ads and has no subscription or in-app purchases.

Privacy

Your messages stay in Android’s own message store, and everything My SMS works out from them — spends, bills, coupons, loans — stays in a private database on your phone, excluded from cloud backup. A backup file is made only when you ask for one. My SMS has no account and no crash reporting. Anonymous usage statistics are off unless you turn them on, and never include message text, amounts, names or numbers. Updates arrive through the Google Play Store. Read the full privacy policy.
