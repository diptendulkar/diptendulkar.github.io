Offline · No sign-up

Repeat it, restyle it, clean it up.

A small text studio for the things people actually do in a chat box: repeat a line a thousand times, turn it into a fancy font, send an empty message, or tidy up text that arrived as a mess. No account, and every tool runs with the network off.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Repeat. Type a word or a line, choose how many times, and get the result ready to copy — up to ten thousand repeats.
  • Fancy fonts. The same sentence rendered in Unicode styles that survive a paste into WhatsApp, Instagram or a bio field.
  • Blank characters. Invisible Unicode you can send as an empty message or use to pad a name.
  • Kaomoji and symbols. Browsable sets, one tap to copy.
  • Counts and cleanup. Character, word and line counts, and tools to strip blank lines and stray spacing out of pasted text.

Everything happens on the phone

The text you type is transformed on the device. There is no server behind the app, no account, and nothing to sign up for. Copy uses Android’s clipboard and sharing uses the system share sheet, so once a line leaves the app it goes only where you sent it.

Privacy

The text you type stays on your phone. The app does show full-screen ads between actions, which is how Google may collect an advertising identifier. Read the full privacy policy.
