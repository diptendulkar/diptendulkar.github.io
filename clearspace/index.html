Storage cleaner · no ads, no subscriptions

Make room. Keep what matters.

ClearSpace shows how full your phone is as a tank of water, then finds what is taking the room: blurry shots, piles of screenshots, copies of the same photo, leftover junk files and apps you no longer open. Nothing is removed until you choose it.

What it does

  • Blurry photos. Each photo is measured for sharpness on the phone, and the out-of-focus and shaken ones are listed softest first.
  • Screenshots. Every screenshot in one place, to clear in a batch.
  • Duplicates and bursts. Exact copies and near-identical shots are grouped. One tap selects every copy except one, and the one kept is always shown.
  • Junk files. Old installer files, temporary files, thumbnail caches, empty folders and folders left by uninstalled apps. Only the safe ones are ticked to start with.
  • Unused apps. Apps grouped by how long since you last opened them — three months, six months, a year — with their size, and uninstalled one after another.
  • App cache. A guided walk through the apps holding the most cache, opening each one's Android settings page so you can clear it there.

What it deliberately does not do

No ads, no subscriptions and nothing to unlock. No account. Anonymous analytics count screen and feature use but never contain a photo name, file path or installed-app identity. Photos deleted from the app go to Android's trash, where they can be restored for 30 days.

Privacy

Photos, file paths and installed-app identities are checked on the phone and never sent. Read the full privacy policy.
