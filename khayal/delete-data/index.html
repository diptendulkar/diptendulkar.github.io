Delete your data

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This page explains how to delete the data held by Khayal (com.dip.khayal), published by Saral Labs Indian Apps (developer: Diptendu Das).

Khayal has no account and no server of ours. Everything you enter is written to a database inside the app's own private storage on your phone, and it is never uploaded to us. That means you do not have to ask us to delete anything — you can delete all of it yourself, at any time, from the phone, and it is gone immediately.

Delete one person and their records

  1. Open Khayal.
  2. Go to Settings → People.
  3. Tap the person you want to remove, then choose Delete and confirm.

This permanently removes that person's name, age and notes, their medicines, their recorded and skipped doses, their vital readings, their appointments and any pill-strip photographs attached to them.

Delete everything

  1. Uninstall Khayal, or
  2. Open Android Settings → Apps → Khayal → Storage → Clear storage.

Either action deletes the entire Khayal database and all attached photographs. Khayal keeps no backup of its own, and it does not sync to any cloud, so there is nothing left behind for us to hold. The app asks you to acknowledge this on first launch, precisely because an uninstall is unrecoverable.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
People, medicines, doses, vital readings, appointments, notes, pill-strip photographs Deleted immediately on the phone by the steps above. Retention period: none — it is removed as soon as you confirm.
Your settings and reminder schedules Deleted when you uninstall or clear storage.
Advertising identifier and basic device information used by Google AdMob to serve non-personalised ads Never held by us. It is handled by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID from Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Google's retention is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
Health data on any server None exists. Khayal never uploads a medicine name, a reading, a patient name or a photograph.

If you would rather ask us

You are welcome to write to diptendu.tu@gmail.com with “Khayal — delete my data” in the subject line. We will reply, but the honest answer will be the steps above: we do not hold a copy of your records, so only your phone can delete them.
