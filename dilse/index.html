Offline · No sign-up

Lines worth posting, in four languages.

Dilse is a WhatsApp status, Hindi shayari and DP app. Copy a line, share it to WhatsApp, or save it for the next festival, birthday or mood. No account, and it works with the network off.

What it does

  • Copy and share in one tap. Every line sits on a card that is already sized for a status post.
  • Eight collections. Status and wishes, attitude, jokes, love, हिंदी शायरी, हिंदी चुटकुले, বাংলা and ਪੰਜਾਬੀ.
  • Status Studio. Turn any line into a picture-status you can post as an image.
  • A Today card for the next Indian festival, so you are not hunting for wishes the night before.
  • Mine. Write your own lines. They stay on the phone.

Built for Indian scripts

Devanagari, Bengali and Gurmukhi are set with enough line height that matras do not collide, and there is a text-size control for smaller screens. Dark by default, with a light paper theme if you prefer it.

Privacy

Favourites and the lines you write never leave your phone. The app does show ads and records anonymous usage. Read the full privacy policy.
