Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Daily Motivational Pic Quotes (“the app”) is an Android collection of motivational picture-quotes, published as com.dip.quotes. This policy explains what we do and do not hold about you, what the advertising software in the app may collect, and how to contact us.

The short version

We run no backend that holds anything about you and you have no account, so we hold no personal information about you and have no way to obtain any.

The app does show ads (Google AdMob). That service is operated by Google, not by us. We do not sell your information.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, and we do not ask you for your name, your email address, your phone number or your location. We do not know which quotes you read, save or share.

Anything the app remembers between launches — such as your favourites and which category you last opened — is stored in the app’s private storage on your own device. Other apps cannot read it, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may use an advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and network information, and your interaction with ads, as described in Google’s advertising privacy policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised advertising, from the ads controls in your Android settings. Where Google requires a consent prompt for personalised ads, including in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, that prompt is shown by Google’s own software rather than by us.

Because the ads are served by Google, we do not choose which ads you see and we do not receive the data Google uses to choose them.

Sharing a quote

Sharing uses Android’s system share sheet. When you share an image it goes to the app you picked — WhatsApp, Instagram, a messaging app — and that app’s own privacy policy covers what happens next. We are not in that path and receive no copy.

The quotes themselves

The quotes are a compiled collection intended as encouragement, not as advice. Nothing in the app is medical, psychological or professional guidance. If you are struggling, please speak to a qualified person rather than an app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy is published here. If it changes, this page is updated and the date above changes with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about privacy in the app: please open an issue at github.com/diptendulkar/diptendulkar.github.io/issues, or email diptendu.tu@gmail.com.
