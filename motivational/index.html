A picture-quote worth posting, every day.

Tens of thousands of motivational and inspirational quotes, already set as images, so there is nothing to design before you post one. A fresh set arrives each day.

What it does

  • Ready-made picture-quotes. Every line comes as an image sized for a status post, so you can share it straight to WhatsApp or Instagram.
  • A new set daily, which is the point — you are not scrolling the same collection you saw last week.
  • Twenty moods to pick from. Inspirational, motivation, life, love, success, positive, happiness, wisdom, attitude, morning, smile, friendship, relationship, depression, students, teachers, athletes, working out, women and men.

Made for sharing

Sharing goes through Android’s own share sheet, so a quote goes to whichever app you pick and nowhere else. There is no account and no profile to set up.

Privacy

The app has no account and we hold no information about you. It does show ads, which is how Google may collect an advertising identifier. Read the full privacy policy.
