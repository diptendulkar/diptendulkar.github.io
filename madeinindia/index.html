Find the Indian alternative before you buy.

A browsable list of Swadeshi and foreign brands set side by side, category by category, so that “be vocal for local” turns into something you can actually check while you are shopping.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Indian and foreign products together. Each category lists both, so you can see what the home-grown option actually is rather than guessing.
  • Organised by category rather than by brand, which is how the question usually comes up — you want a soap or a phone, not a particular company.
  • Built around Aatmanirbhar Bharat. The point is to make the self-reliance argument practical at the shelf.

मेड इन इंडिया (स्वदेशी) और विदेशी उत्पादों की सूची — स्थानीय के साथ-साथ अपने देश को आत्मनिर्भर बनाने के लिए मुखर हों।
