Free · On-device question bank

Mock tests for bank exams, SSC, UPSC and interviews.

SkillZora is a free mock-test and flashcard app. Sit a 15-minute paper, swipe interview cards, and see which answers you got wrong. The question bank ships on the phone. No account.

What it does

  • Timed mock tests. Twenty random questions, fifteen minutes, then a score with every right and wrong answer marked.
  • Practice by topic. Java Exception Handling, Design Patterns, IBPS Reasoning, SSC Quant — drill one chapter, or mix the whole paper.
  • Tracks you can hide. Banking, SSC, Railways, UPSC, Defence, Insurance, or Technical IT. Tick what you care about in Settings.
  • Progress. Questions attempted, tests taken, accuracy and best score.

What is in the bank

IBPS PO and Clerk, SBI PO, RBI Grade B, SSC CGL and CHSL, RRB NTPC, UPSC Prelims, NDA, LIC AAO, and interview decks for Java, Spring, Python, JavaScript, Android, SQL and more. Thousands of questions, shuffled each session.

Privacy

Scores and streaks stay on the phone. The app is free and shows ads on Home and after a timed test, never on a card you are answering. Read the full privacy policy.
