Video & photo editor · on-device, no watermark

Videos, photos and posters, all in one app.

ClipQ is an all-in-one video and photo editor. Edit a long video for your YouTube channel, turn its highlight into a Short or reel, design the thumbnail and touch up your photos, all on your phone. Every tool says what it does in plain words, and nothing you save carries a watermark.

What it does

  • Cut and arrange. Trim, split, cut out the middle, freeze a frame, reorder clips, reverse a clip, and change speed from 0.1x slow motion to 8x, or up to 10x on a speed curve.
  • Look. Filters with a strength slider, brightness, contrast, warmth and more, or your own .cube colour files.
  • Text and stickers. Drag captions and emoji onto the video, pinch to resize, and choose when each one appears.
  • Sound. Add songs from your phone, record a voiceover, change your voice, and drop in sound effects from the built-in library.
  • Motion. Transitions between clips, timeline effects, and keyframes that move, zoom or turn a clip over time.
  • Overlays. Put a second video on top as picture-in-picture, side by side or top and bottom, and remove a green or blue screen behind it.
  • Photos and posters. 54 photo looks with a before and after view, 30 poster and thumbnail templates, and collages where you pop out of the frame.
  • Teleprompter and captions. Read your script while you record, and add captions timed word by word, in Hindi, Hinglish and many more languages.
  • AutoCut. Pick photos or clips and a song, and ClipQ cuts them to the beat.
  • Save. Up to 1080p on most phones and up to 4K where the phone can encode it, at 24, 30 or 60 fps.

What it deliberately does not do

No account, no sign-in, no watermark, no subscription and nothing to unlock. ClipQ shows no ads, and editing and saving work with no connection. It counts which tools get used, so we know what to build next, but analytics never sees your photos or videos.

Privacy

Your photos, videos, music and voice recordings stay on the phone. ClipQ shows your gallery inside the app only if you allow it, opens only the clips you add, uses the microphone only while you record a voiceover, and uses the camera only in the teleprompter. Read the full privacy policy.
