Video editor · on-device, no watermark

Make reels and status videos in a few taps.

ClipQ is a video editor built for beginners. Pick videos and photos, and the editor puts them on one timeline you can trim, speed up, colour and caption. Everything happens on your phone, and the finished video carries no watermark.

What it does

  • Cut and arrange. Trim, split, cut out the middle, freeze a frame, reorder clips, reverse a clip, and change speed from 0.1x slow motion to 8x.
  • Look. Filters with a strength slider, brightness, contrast, warmth and more, or your own .cube colour files.
  • Text and stickers. Drag captions and emoji onto the video, pinch to resize, and choose when each one appears.
  • Sound. Add songs from your phone, record a voiceover, change your voice, and drop in sound effects from the built-in library.
  • Motion. Transitions between clips, timeline effects, and keyframes that move, zoom or turn a clip over time.
  • Overlays. Put a second video on top as picture-in-picture, side by side or top and bottom, and remove a green or blue screen behind it.
  • Photo status. Pick photos and a song, and ClipQ changes photo on the beat.
  • Save. Up to 1080p on most phones and up to 4K where the phone can encode it, at 24, 30 or 60 fps.

What it deliberately does not do

No account, no sign-in, no watermark, no subscription and nothing to unlock. ClipQ shows no ads, and editing and saving work with no connection. It counts which tools get used, so we know what to build next, but never sees your photos or videos.

Privacy

Your photos, videos, music and voice recordings stay on the phone. ClipQ only sees the items you pick with Android’s photo picker, uses the microphone only while you record a voiceover, and uses the camera only in the teleprompter. Read the full privacy policy.
