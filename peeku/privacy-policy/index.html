Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Peeku is a learning app for children aged 3 to 7, made by Saral Labs (Diptendu Das). It has no ads, no subscriptions and nothing to buy. This policy explains what the app keeps, what it sends, and how to remove it.

What stays on the phone

  • Child profiles: an optional first name, a chosen animal avatar and a prompt language. Up to three profiles.
  • Progress: which worksheets were finished, the stars earned, the number of tries and the time taken.
  • Settings: the parent-zone language, volume limit, reduce motion, vibration and the usage-report choice.

These are stored in the app's private storage. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and they are excluded from Google cloud backup.

What is sent, and when

Anonymous usage and crash reports, only if a parent turns them on. At setup, and at any time in the parent zone, a parent can allow anonymous reports through Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They are off until a parent says yes. When on, they contain:

  • which worksheets are started, finished or left early, their template, class and subject, stars, hints shown, the number of wrong tries, how far through a worksheet left early got, whether it was finished for the first time, and seconds taken;
  • how far a child has got in a subject, sent once each at 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, every 50 after that and all of a subject's worksheets, as a count only (for example “25 of 100 in EVS, UKG”), so we can see where more worksheets are needed;
  • which screens are opened and which class and subject are chosen;
  • parent-zone actions: settings changed (such as volume, language or reduce motion), a profile added or removed, a worksheet reopened from the progress page, the app shared or the Play listing opened, an update offered or accepted;
  • at setup, the prompt language, the avatar picked and whether a name was entered (yes or no, never the name);
  • the type of device (phone, tablet or TV), Android version and app version;
  • crash details, if the app crashes.

They never contain the child's name, avatar or anything typed. The advertising ID, ad personalisation and the Android SSAID are switched off and the advertising-ID permission is removed from the app.

The minimum-version check, always. On launch the app asks Firebase Remote Config for the lowest app version that is still safe to use, and which classes (such as LKG) are switched on. This request carries no information about the child and is made whether or not reports are on. It exists so that a version with a serious bug can be asked to update, and so a class already inside the app can be opened or closed without an update.

Google Play services. Each time the app opens it asks Google Play whether a newer version exists, and Google Play may show its own update screen. After the app has been opened a few times, Google Play's review sheet may be shown to a parent on entering the parent zone, after the gate; it is never shown on the child's screens. Sharing the app sends only the Play Store link, through the app the parent picks.

Google's handling of this data is described in the Google privacy policy and Firebase privacy information.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for the reports above, the minimum-version check, and Google Play's update and review services.
  • Vibrate: a short buzz when a child taps a button. A parent can switch it off.
  • Wake lock: lets Firebase finish sending a report if the screen turns off. Only used when reports are on.

The app does not ask for the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or the advertising ID.

Speech

Prompts are spoken by the text-to-speech engine already on the phone. Peeku does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Children

Peeku is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families policy. It does not show ads, does not collect personal information from children, and keeps every setting that matters behind a parent gate (a multiplication question with the numbers written as words).

Removing data

In the parent zone a parent can delete one child's profile and progress, or choose Delete all data to remove every profile, all progress and all settings. Uninstalling the app also removes everything stored on the phone. Anonymous reports already sent cannot be linked to a child; to ask about them, email us.
