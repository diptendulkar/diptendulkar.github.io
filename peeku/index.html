Learning for ages 3 to 7 · no ads, nothing to buy

Short worksheets, read aloud.

Peeku is a learning app for children aged 3 to 7, starting with UKG. Peeku the peacock reads every instruction aloud in English, Hindi or Kannada, so a child who cannot read yet can still play on their own.

What it does

  • Eight subjects. Maths, English, Hindi, Kannada, EVS, logic, good habits and music, as a trail of short worksheets with stars.
  • Letters with pictures. A whole alphabet on one screen: tap अ, ಅ or A and see its picture and word, read aloud.
  • Spelling and numbers. A–Z words, three- and four-letter spelling, and number names from one to a hundred in groups of ten.
  • Sa Re Ga Ma. A xylophone that names and sounds each note, and short tunes to play back.
  • Up to three children. Each with their own name, animal friend, language and progress.
  • A parent zone behind a gate. Volume limit, reduce motion, vibration, languages and the usage-report choice, reached by a question a young child cannot answer.

What it deliberately does not do

No ads, no subscriptions, no account and nothing to buy. Every worksheet is inside the app, so it plays without a connection. Anonymous usage and crash reports are off until a parent turns them on, and never include the child's name.
