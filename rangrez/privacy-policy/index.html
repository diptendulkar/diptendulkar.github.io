Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Rangrez is a camera app made by Saral Labs, published on Google Play as com.dip.rangrez. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your photos, videos, voice and scripts never leave your phone.

What Rangrez uses

  • Your camera, while the camera screen is open. Looks, grain, skin smoothing and every other effect are applied on your phone as the picture is made.
  • Your microphone, only while you record a Short or a video. Rangrez asks the first time you record, and records silent video if you decline.
  • .cube colour files you choose to import. Rangrez reads only the file you pick.

Rangrez does not ask for access to your photo library. It can show you only the photos and videos it saved itself.

What Rangrez stores, and where

  • Photos and videos you take are saved to your phone’s gallery in the DCIM/Rangrez folder. They are yours to keep, share or delete like any other photo.
  • Clips of a Short in progress are kept in Rangrez’s private storage until you save or discard the Short, and are cleared the next time the app starts.
  • Imported looks are copied into Rangrez’s private storage.
  • Your settings and teleprompter script are kept in Rangrez’s private storage.

Uninstalling Rangrez removes everything in its private storage. Photos and videos already saved to your gallery stay there.

Rangrez has no account and no sign-in.

What Rangrez sends over the internet

Nothing you shoot or write. Taking, grading, joining and saving photos and videos all happen on your phone and work with no connection.

What does go over the network:

  • Advertising, described in the next section.
  • In-app updates and reviews. Google Play checks whether a newer version of Rangrez is available, and may show Play’s rating sheet. Google handles this under Google’s privacy policy.

Rangrez does not use analytics or crash-reporting services. We do not sell your information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Rangrez shows ads to help cover its costs.

  • A banner may appear in the Gallery and on the Short review screen only. There are no ads on the camera screen and no full-screen ads.
  • Every ad request asks for non-personalised ads. Ads are never chosen using your photos, videos or scripts, and none of them are sent to advertisers.
  • Google AdMob may still receive your device’s advertising ID (the app declares AD_ID), an approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, as described in How Google uses information from apps and Google’s advertising policy.
  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google’s consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Backups

Rangrez opts out of Android backup and device transfer, so its settings, scripts and imported looks stay on this phone. Photos and videos in your gallery are backed up by your gallery app or Google Photos if you use one.
