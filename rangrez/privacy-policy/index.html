Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

HD Camera (formerly Rangrez) is a camera app made by Saral Labs, published on Google Play as com.dip.rangrez. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your photos, videos, voice and scripts never leave your phone, and your location is used only for the stamps and tags you turn on.

What HD Camera uses

  • Your camera, while the camera screen is open. Looks, filters, Pro settings, HDR, night and other scenes, skin smoothing, live effects, Restyle art styles, Layouts and panoramas are all made on your phone.
  • Where faces are in the picture, only while an effect that follows a face (such as the flower crown) or beauty is on. A small copy of the viewfinder is checked for faces several times a second by Google’s ML Kit face detection, which runs entirely on your phone. Only the position and size of each face are used; nothing about a face is stored, and no picture is sent anywhere.
  • Your location, only while a place watermark (Travel diary or Place) or Location tags is on, and only after you allow it. See Location below.
  • The gyroscope, while you shoot a panorama or look around one, to follow how far the phone has turned. These readings are used on the spot and never stored.
  • Photos and videos you choose to edit, restyle, trim or put in a collage. Edits are saved as a new copy; the original is not changed.
  • Your microphone, only while you record a video or a Short. HD Camera asks the first time you record, and records silent video if you decline.
  • .cube colour files you choose to import. HD Camera reads only the file you pick.

HD Camera does not ask for access to your photo library. It can show you only the photos and videos it saved itself.

Location

Location is off until you turn on a place watermark or Location tags, and Android asks your permission first. You can allow approximate or precise location, or decline; the camera works either way.

While it is on and the camera screen is open, HD Camera reads the phone’s position every 20 seconds or so and uses it to:

  • print the place, and for Travel diary the coordinates, on the photo when you choose one of those watermarks, and
  • save the coordinates inside the photo or video file (as standard GPS tags) when Location tags is on.

To turn coordinates into a place name such as “Paris, France”, HD Camera uses Android’s built-in geocoder. On most phones this service is provided by Google Play services (or by the phone’s maker), which may send the coordinates to its own servers to look the name up, under that provider’s privacy policy. HD Camera itself never sends your location anywhere, keeps no location history, and stops reading the location when you leave the camera.

The location saved in a photo or video goes wherever that file goes. If you share a photo with Location tags on, the person you share it with can see where it was taken. Turn Location tags off before shooting anything you want to keep private.

What HD Camera stores, and where

  • Photos and videos you take or edit are saved to your phone’s gallery in the DCIM/HD Camera folder, with each trip in its own album under DCIM/HD Camera/Trips. They are yours to keep, share or delete like any other photo. Photos from before 2.0 stay in DCIM/Rangrez.
  • Trips: the name, start and end dates and album of each trip, kept in HD Camera’s private storage.
  • Your signature for watermarks, your settings and your teleprompter script, kept in HD Camera’s private storage.
  • Clips of a Short or a Layout in progress are kept in private storage until you save or discard them, and are cleared the next time the app starts.
  • Imported looks are copied into private storage.

Uninstalling HD Camera removes everything in its private storage. Photos and videos already saved to your gallery stay there.

When you delete photos or videos in HD Camera, Android may ask you to confirm; they are then removed from your phone.

HD Camera has no account and no sign-in.

What HD Camera sends over the internet

Nothing you shoot, edit or write. Taking, grading, stitching, editing, joining and saving photos and videos all happen on your phone and work with no connection.

What does go over the network:

  • Advertising, described in the next section.
  • Place names, through Android’s geocoder, as described under Location, only while a place watermark or Location tags is on.
  • In-app updates and reviews. Google Play checks whether a newer version of HD Camera is available, and may show Play’s rating sheet. Google handles this under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Face detection diagnostics. Google’s ML Kit library, which finds faces for the face effects and beauty, sends Google technical information about how the library performs: device model and Android version, the app’s package name and version, a per-installation identifier that is not meant to identify you or your phone, speed, settings and error codes. It never includes the picture or anything about the faces in it. Google uses it for diagnostics and usage analytics and does not pass it to third parties, as described in ML Kit’s data disclosure.

HD Camera itself does not use analytics or crash-reporting services. We do not sell your information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

HD Camera shows ads to help cover its costs.

  • A banner may appear in the Gallery and on the Short review screen only. There are no ads on the camera screen or in the editor, and no full-screen ads.
  • Every ad request asks for non-personalised ads. Ads are never chosen using your photos, videos, scripts or location, and none of them are sent to advertisers.
  • Google AdMob may still receive your device’s advertising ID (the app declares AD_ID), an approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, as described in How Google uses information from apps and Google’s advertising policy.
  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google’s consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Backups

HD Camera opts out of Android backup and device transfer, so its settings, trips, scripts and imported looks stay on this phone. Photos and videos in your gallery are backed up by your gallery app or Google Photos if you use one.
