Travel diary · Pro · Pano · Editor

An easy camera for travel.

HD Camera (formerly Rangrez) is a camera app for Android. Shoot in full resolution with pro controls when you want them, stamp the place and time on your photos, keep every trip in its own diary, and edit everything in one place. Big buttons and plain words. Photos and videos are made on the phone and saved to your gallery; nothing you shoot is uploaded.

What it does

  • Travel diary. Start a trip and every photo and video goes into its own album, sorted by day. Make a memory video or a collage from a trip in one tap.
  • Stamps and watermarks. The trip name, time, date and GPS coordinates on a glass card, or the place name, the time, a film-style date or your own signature.
  • Pro mode. ISO, shutter speed, white balance, exposure, focus and AE/AF lock, with scenes for night, sports, sunset and more, and HDR. Which controls appear depends on what the phone’s camera supports.
  • Panorama. Turn slowly and the view is joined into one wide photo; look around it later by turning the phone or dragging.
  • Photo and video. Zoom bar and pinch zoom, tap to focus, golden-ratio and other grids, timer, burst, volume-key shutter, silent shutter where the law allows it, stabilization and time-lapse.
  • Filters and looks. Film looks with a strength slider, live in the viewfinder, and your own .cube colour files.
  • Beauty and effects. Gentle skin smoothing, a front-camera glow, live effects such as a flower crown or snowfall, and art styles like cartoon and sketch.
  • Photo editor. Crop, rotate, flip and mirror; brightness, contrast, warmth, fade and vignette; filters, stickers, text, doodles, mosaic and blur, and frames. Trim videos.
  • Gallery and clean-up. Photos, videos, panoramas and trips in their own tabs. Select many to share, make a collage, or delete and see the space freed.

Where ads appear

A small banner may appear in the Gallery and on the short video review screen. There are no ads on the camera screen or in the editor, and no full-screen ads. Every ad request asks for non-personalised ads.

Privacy

Photos, videos, your voice and your scripts never leave the phone, and faces are found on the phone too. Location is used only for the stamps and tags you turn on. HD Camera has no account, and no analytics or crash reporting of its own; Google’s face detection library sends Google its own diagnostics, never pictures. It needs the internet for ads, place names and Google Play updates. Read the full privacy policy.
