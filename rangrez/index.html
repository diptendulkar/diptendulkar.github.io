Film looks · Shorts · On-device

A camera that dyes the picture as you take it.

Rangrez is a camera app for Android. Film looks, grain, halation and light leaks are applied live in the viewfinder, so what you see is what you save. Photos and videos are graded on the phone and saved to your gallery; nothing you shoot is uploaded.

What it does

  • Film looks. Swipe across the viewfinder to change the look — Natural, a 2005 digicam, Cinematic 800T, a disposable with a date stamp, Mono 400 and more. Each look mixes its own grain, halation, colour fringing and vignette, and some add light leaks.
  • Tone pad. Drag one dot to move the picture warmer or cooler and from clean to grainy, without opening a menu.
  • Skin and Glow. Gentle skin smoothing, and on the front camera a ring of screen light whose colour you can set, for faces in dim rooms.
  • Shorts. Record 15 or 60 seconds in segments, at slow or fast speed, with the last frame shown faintly as a ghost so the next shot lines up. Undo the last segment, then join and save the Short on the phone.
  • Teleprompter. A scrolling script near the lens for Shorts and videos.
  • Your own looks. Import a .cube colour file and use it like any built-in look.

Where ads appear

A small banner may appear in the Gallery and on the Short review screen. There are no ads on the camera screen and no full-screen ads. Every ad request asks for non-personalised ads.

Privacy

Photos, videos, your voice and your scripts never leave the phone. Rangrez has no account, no analytics and no crash reporting. It needs the internet for ads and Google Play updates. Read the full privacy policy.
